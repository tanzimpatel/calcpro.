CalcPro Security Lab

Practice defensive security in a safe, local-only environment.

AUTHORIZED TRAINING ONLY

Rules of engagement

  • Use these exercises only on systems you own or have explicit permission to test.
  • This lab does not scan networks, attack websites, exploit real targets, or send traffic to third parties.
  • Use fictional data. Never enter real passwords, tokens, or private information.

1. Password hygiene

Learn why length and uniqueness matter. The lab checks locally and never sends your text anywhere.

Enter a fictional password to begin.

2. Security-header review

Paste headers from a system you are authorized to review. This checker stays in your browser.

Recommended headers: CSP, HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, and Permissions-Policy.

3. Threat-model checklist

Identify defensive controls before releasing a website.

Complete the checklist to assess readiness.
Local training tool. It provides educational checks, not a penetration test or a guarantee of security.
